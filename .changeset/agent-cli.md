Drive Peony from scripts and AI agents with the new `peony` command: `peony list`, `peony status`, `peony connect <name>` and `peony disconnect`, with `--json` output and exit codes that say what happened. `connect` waits until the VPN is on and your hosts are checked, and Peony stays the only thing that starts or stops a tunnel.
